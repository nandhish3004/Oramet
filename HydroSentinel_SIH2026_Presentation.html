HYDROSENTINEL Proposed Solution & Idea Approach
SIH 2026 | PS #26192

1. Multi-Source Sensor & Telemetry Ingestion

  • IMD AWS & ARGs: Real-time panchayat-level precipitation intensity, humidity, and barometric pressure.
  • NHAI RWIS Sensors: Road surface friction index (μ), water film depth, and mountain highway washouts.
  • NASA SMAP / ISRO MOSDAC: Satellite root-zone soil saturation % to detect exhausted runoff absorption capacity.
  • GSI Topography DEM: Slope angles (>30°) and digital elevation gradient analysis.

2. Hydrological Lag-Time & Geotechnical Risk Model

  • Lead-Time Calculator: Actionable minutes remaining until maximum flood peak crest.
  • Factor of Safety (Fs): Geotechnical thresholding to preemptively flag shallow landslides.
  • 4-Factor Composite Hazard: 40% Rain Departure + 25% Soil Saturation + 20% Slope + 15% RWIS Road Status.

3. Resilient Safe Evacuation & Emergency SOS

  • High-Ground Walking Routes: Directs citizens strictly away from riverbeds via Google Maps.
  • Dual-Channel SOS: REST API online with zero-delay fallback to native SMS 112 with exact coordinates.
  • Shake-to-SOS & 3.5 kHz Siren: Accelerometer trigger for wet/cold hands & acoustic whistle for K9 search teams.
